*Episode 10 of 13 in a video tour of [Git Gud](https://github.com/joe-lloyd/git-gud), a desktop Git client with a lane-colored commit graph. The video is above; this is what it says, with the details.*

The gear opens Settings. Text size scales the whole interface, from 70% to 180%.

High contrast brightens text and borders, for glare or tired eyes.

Auto-fix blocked actions and reuse recorded resolutions are here, which we met in the conflicts episode.

Gerrit mode has its settings here per repository, along with the Gerrit login, which is stored encrypted.

Sharing with other Git Gud instances lives here too. That's the next episode.

## Updates

Under Updates, choose a channel. Stable gets releases. Dev gets pre-releases as soon as they're built.

Git Gud checks for updates on its own. When one is ready, a button in the tab bar shows the download, and then offers to restart into the new version.

The version you're running is always in the corner of the tab bar.
